PRIVACY / WORKING DRAFT

Privacy policy.

Draft · November 11, 2026. This policy is a working draft and has not been legally reviewed. Hosting and operator details must be confirmed before publication.

This draft describes the current static Fit Tetra concept website.

The website experience

The website displays clothing concepts and an optional interactive 3D preview. It does not provide accounts, checkout, user uploads, camera access, community posting or email collection forms.

Analytics, cookies & storage

The website code does not include analytics, advertising trackers or third-party font services. It does not set cookies or store preferences in local storage. Your browser may cache the website’s files.

3D files & device preferences

The interactive preview uses locally hosted JavaScript and model files. Rendering takes place in your browser. The code reads your reduced-motion preference and, where supported, your data-saving preference to choose a lighter experience. Those preferences are not sent by the website code to an analytics service.

Hosting requests

Like other websites, loading these pages requires requests to the hosting server. The hosting provider may receive technical information such as IP addresses, requested URLs, browser information and request times. The provider, logging practices and retention periods have not yet been confirmed for this draft and must be completed before publication.

When you email us

Clicking an email link opens your email application. If you choose to send a message to contact@fittetra.com, your email address, message and any information you include will be handled through email services. The mailbox provider, access practices and retention periods must be confirmed before publication. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for your inquiry.

Operator details & applicable rights

The operator’s legal name, address, relevant jurisdictions and applicable privacy-rights process must be completed before publication. This draft does not make claims about legal compliance or legal approval.

Contact

For privacy questions, email contact@fittetra.com.

Changes to this draft

This policy will need updating if the website’s features, hosting or data practices change.
