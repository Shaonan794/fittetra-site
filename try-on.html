Photo guide / A helpful starting point

A few angles.
No perfect pose.

Your camera roll is a good place to start. These four views show useful angles; we’re testing how few photos are needed before setting the upload requirements.

Front

Face forward, with ears and paws in the picture.

Side

Show the whole body, including the paws and tail.

Back

Take a full-body view from behind.

Above

Show the outline of the back from above.

Use a comfortable, natural stance. If a view is missing, the plan is to ask for that view—not make you start over.

A little clarity.

Can I try this without an account?

Yes. Explore all three outfit concepts and the base model without signing in. For future personal try-on, we plan to let you see your result before deciding whether to sign in and save it.

Can I upload my dog’s photos today?

Not yet. This preview demonstrates the model experience and explains what we’re building. There is no photo upload or personal generation in this version.

What does the first free preview include?

The planned offer is one successfully generated 3D outfit preview, with one supported clothing choice. It isn’t an unlimited wardrobe or unlimited regeneration. Launch details will be shown before you submit photos.

Can I use static images instead?

Yes. Select Use static view. Reduced motion keeps the experience in static images. Static previews are also shown for supported data-saving settings or when 3D is unavailable.

Can I use this to choose a size?

Sizing & fit: coming soon.
